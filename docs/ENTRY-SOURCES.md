# Entry rules: what they're based on (checked Oct 2026, re-checked 23 Oct 2026)

Re-check these before launch and every few months. They are a guide, always confirm on the official site.

- **Thailand**: 60-day visa-free scheme ended 14 Sep 2026; from 15 Sep 2026 tourists from the US, UK, Australia, Canada, EU etc. get 30 days (extendable 30 days in country, ~1,900 THB; or a 60-day TR e-visa). TDAC arrival card still required. Land entries under the exemption capped at 2 per calendar year. Sources: visasnews.com, theo-courant.com, itij.com reporting.
- **Vietnam**: 45-day visa-free for UK, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Denmark, Sweden, Finland (to Mar 2028) and, for tourism, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Czechia, Hungary, Luxembourg, Netherlands, Poland, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Switzerland (to Aug 2028). US / Canada / Australia: e-visa, ~$25, 90 days. Official portal: evisa.gov.vn. Sources: vietnamtourism.com, vnembassy pages.
- **Indonesia**: VOA / e-VOA IDR 500,000 (~$30-35), 30 days, extendable once. Free All Indonesia Arrival Card. Bali tourist levy IDR 150,000. Mad Monkey's own guide: madmonkeyhostels.com/blogs/indonesia-visa-requirements/.
- **Cambodia**: $30 e-visa or VOA, 30 days, extendable once (~$45). (Some sites still show the old $36 e-visa.)
- **Laos**: VOA or e-visa; reported fees range ~$30-55 across sources (unclear): verify. Electronic arrival form required (immigration.gov.la).
- **Philippines**: 30 days visa-free for US/UK/CA/AU/most EU; free eTravel within 72h before arrival; onward ticket. Mad Monkey's own guide: madmonkeyhostels.com/blogs/philippines-visa-guide-tourist-entry-requirements/.

Where sources conflicted (Laos fees, Vietnam re-entry rules) the app says "check the official figure" rather than guessing.

## Re-check, 23 Oct 2026 (the big review)

Each headline rule was searched again and still matches: Thailand's cut to 30 days from 15 Sep 2026 (Royal Gazette, 31 Aug 2026; land entries limited to two a calendar year; one extension at an officer's discretion), Vietnam's 45-day exemption for UK passports to 14 Mar 2028 and the $25 / 90-day e-visa for everyone else, Cambodia's $30 e-visa (cut from $36 on 1 Jan 2025) with a $45 extension, Indonesia's IDR 500,000 VOA with the free All Indonesia Arrival Card and the IDR 150,000 Bali levy, the Philippines' 30 days visa-free with free eTravel registration within 72 hours, and Laos's still-disputed fee ($30-55 depending on the source). Nothing needed changing. Sources were news and travel sites, not the governments' own pages, which is why the app keeps saying "confirm on the official site". Not verified: Mad Monkey Kampot's opening date (the data says 1 Nov 2026; Mad Monkey announced a riverside resort for late 2025, so ask the team whether it is open now).
