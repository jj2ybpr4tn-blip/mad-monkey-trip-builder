Festivals and closures to plan around

  • Christmas + New Year peak Sun 20 Dec to Tue 5 Jan

    The busiest and dearest weeks of the year across the region. Popular hostels, island boats and flights sell out. Book early and avoid one-night stops. It is worth paying for a bed you have held.

  • Nyepi (Bali Day of Silence) 9 Mar, Mon 8 Mar to Wed 10 Mar

    Bali shuts down for 24 hours. The airport closes, nobody goes outside, and there is no traffic or noise. Nusa Lembongan observes it too. Be in your hostel the day before and don't book a flight or boat that day. It can be a very peaceful day. Confirm the date locally.
