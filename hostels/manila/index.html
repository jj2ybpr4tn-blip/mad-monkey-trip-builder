Festivals and closures to plan around

  • Christmas + New Year peak Sun 20 Dec to Tue 5 Jan

    The busiest and dearest weeks of the year across the region. Popular hostels, island boats and flights sell out. Book early and avoid one-night stops. It is worth paying for a bed you have held.

  • Holy Week (Semana Santa) Sun 21 Mar to Mon 29 Mar

    The Philippines nearly stops from Maundy Thursday to Easter. Transport is jammed, many shops and bars shut, and some places don't sell alcohol. Siquijor has its famous Holy Week healing festival. Book ferries and flights early and have a plan for Thursday and Friday.
