Local knowledge

  • Rinjani treks are usually closed from about January to March. Check before you book.
  • The surf is for people with some experience. Beginners should book lessons at the gentler breaks.
  • Roads outside town are rough. Ride carefully or take a driver.

Festivals and closures to plan around

  • Christmas + New Year peak Sun 20 Dec to Tue 5 Jan

    The busiest and dearest weeks of the year across the region. Popular hostels, island boats and flights sell out. Book early and avoid one-night stops. It is worth paying for a bed you have held.

  • Ramadan + Eid (Lebaran) 8 Feb – 9/10 Mar, Mon 8 Feb to Fri 12 Mar

    A Muslim-majority part of Indonesia. Some daytime places open late or close, and the days around Eid (Lebaran) are the busiest travel days of the year, so boats and flights are packed. Respect fasting hours in public, book boats and flights early around Eid, and expect a quieter, more local feel.
